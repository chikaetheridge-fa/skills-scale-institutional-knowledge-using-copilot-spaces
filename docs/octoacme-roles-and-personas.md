# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test coverage, and acceptance criteria validation. They partner with Product, Engineering, and Project teams to ensure features meet agreed quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test strategy and plans for features and releases
- Define acceptance criteria and quality gates with product and engineering teams
- Execute manual and automated testing and coordinate with developers on coverage gaps
- Identify and triage quality issues; track defects through resolution
- Conduct smoke tests and user acceptance testing before production release
- Maintain test infrastructure, automation tools, and reporting

### Goals
- Ensure high quality and reliability of released features
- Reduce defect escape rate to production
- Enable faster feedback cycles through efficient testing

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement to define test criteria
- Daily standups for critical quality blockers and test status
- PR reviews and test result reporting
- Pre-release and post-deploy verification sign-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate code quality and test coverage
- Collaborates with Product Managers to confirm acceptance criteria are measurable and testable
- Supports Project Managers with release readiness and risk visibility
- Coordinates with Technical Leads on environment readiness, release quality gates, and technical risk review

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads shape the technical direction of projects, own architecture decisions, and guide the engineering team on design and technical risk management.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Mentor developers on design patterns, code quality, and best practices
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Collaborate with Product Managers on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Ensure scalability, maintainability, and security of implemented solutions
- Represent engineering concerns in planning and prioritization discussions

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and unplanned rework
- Foster a culture of engineering excellence

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Planning sessions for technical feasibility assessment
- Risk register and dependency mapping
- Code reviews and mentoring feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on solution trade-offs and sequencing
- Guides Developers on architecture, standards, and implementation quality
- Works with Project Managers to surface blockers, dependencies, and schedule impacts
- Supports QA/Testing Lead by clarifying technical constraints and risk areas that affect validation

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are executive or business stakeholders who provide strategic direction, approval authority, and help remove organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter, budget, and resource allocation
- Provide business context and strategic alignment
- Remove cross-team or organizational blockers
- Attend milestone reviews and provide executive feedback
- Serve as escalation point for Level 3 issues or decisions
- Monitor project health and business impact metrics

### Goals
- Ensure projects align with business strategy and deliver measurable ROI
- Remove organizational barriers to successful delivery
- Provide strategic oversight without micromanaging execution

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and charter review
- Monthly or milestone-based executive updates
- Escalation and approval requests
- Post-release business impact review

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Empowers Product Managers and Project Managers with strategic direction and decision authority
- Reviews progress and outcomes with PMs and leadership teams
- Escalates cross-functional issues that need executive intervention
- Aligns business priorities with Product, Delivery, and Engineering decisions

---

## Operations / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Operations and DevOps Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, production monitoring, and operational support. They enable reliable, repeatable releases and healthy runtime performance.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Manage production infrastructure, scaling, and monitoring
- Coordinate deployments and manage rollback procedures
- Respond to production incidents and post-incident reviews
- Maintain runbooks, observability dashboards, and alerting
- Collaborate with development teams on performance and reliability concerns

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and repeatable deployments
- Minimize production incidents and MTTR (mean time to recovery)
- Ensure system reliability and performance at scale

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Infrastructure and dependency discussions during planning
- Post-deploy verification and monitoring
- Incident response and postmortems

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to support deployment readiness and infrastructure requirements
- Partners with QA/Testing Leads on smoke tests, staging validation, and release verification
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release windows, rollback plans, and operational risk
- Supports Product and Sponsor stakeholders by ensuring operational health and deployment confidence

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on agile principles, estimation, and process improvement
- Maintain project documentation, boards, and visibility
- Help resolve conflicts and foster psychological safety
- Track and drive action items from retrospectives

### Goals
- Enable team velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of transparency and continuous improvement
- Reduce friction and unplanned dependencies

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members as needed
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Process improvement proposals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Supports Project Managers by improving team flow and reducing delivery friction
- Partners with Product Managers to improve backlog hygiene and prioritization clarity
- Works with Developers to keep ceremonies effective and impediments visible
- Helps QA and technical stakeholders align on test planning, dependency management, and risk reduction

---

## UX / Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads lead user research, design direction, and usability validation to ensure features deliver excellent user experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Collaborate with product and engineering on design decisions and trade-offs
- Validate solutions against user needs and accessibility standards
- Provide design reviews and feedback to development teams
- Measure and iterate on user experience metrics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden through good design
- Ensure alignment between user needs and implemented solutions

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and requirements discussions
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User testing and validation reporting
- Sprint planning for design dependencies and constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate customer needs into clear experience outcomes
- Aligns with Developers and Technical Leads on feasibility, constraints, and implementation quality
- Supports QA/Testing by defining usability and accessibility validation criteria
- Provides Project Managers with design dependency visibility and milestone impact information

---

## How these roles fit within the OctoAcme project model
These additional personas strengthen the existing framework by clarifying the cross-functional responsibilities required for successful planning, execution, and release. They complement the existing Developer, Product Manager, and Project Manager roles without replacing them:
- Developers build and deliver solutions
- Product Managers define value and priorities
- Project Managers coordinate execution and communication
- QA/Testing Leads protect quality and release readiness
- Technical Leads guide architecture and technical risk
- Stakeholders/Sponsors provide strategic oversight and escalation authority
- Operations/DevOps Engineers ensure deployment reliability and operational health
- Scrum Masters/Agile Coaches improve team flow and delivery discipline
- UX/Design Leads keep customer experience at the center of decision-making

This fuller role model creates clearer ownership, better communication patterns, and more consistent project execution across the lifecycle.
